## Disruptive Digital Solutions Group (D2SG)

**Venture studio.** We work with founders and investors to take ideas from first conversation to product in market, and we build and run products of our own.

- **Founders** bring an idea or an early product. We shape the thesis together, then design, build and launch the product with them, and stay involved as it grows.
- **Investors** bring a thesis or a market they want to back. We turn it into a working product and a business they can evaluate and grow.

**Fast, affordable, exceptional.** Building used to mean choosing two. By working in parallel, automating routine work and building testing in from the start, we deliver all three.
